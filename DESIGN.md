# Terra — Organic Design System

## North Star: "Rooted Warmth"
Calm, grounded, and human. Earthy tones, soft shapes, and natural textures create a warm, approachable experience for professional trades and builders.

## Colors
- **Primary:** `#4a7c59` (Forest Green) — Used for primary actions, success states, and brand highlights.
- **Surface:** `#fdfcfb` (Warm White) — The primary background color, providing a soft, paper-like feel.
- **Surface-Dim:** `#f2efe9` (Earthy Sand) — Used for section backgrounds and secondary containers.
- **On-Surface:** `#2c332e` (Deep Charcoal) — High-contrast text color for maximum legibility.
- **Accent:** `#8b5e34` (Terracotta) — Used for secondary accents and subtle highlights.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Nunito Sans`
- **Scale:**
  - **Display:** 48px / 1.2 line-height — For hero headlines.
  - **Headline:** 32px / 1.3 line-height — For section titles.
  - **Body:** 16px / 1.5 line-height — For general content.
  - **Caption:** 14px / 1.4 line-height — For secondary metadata.

## Shapes & Spacing
- **Roundness:** `ROUND_TWELVE` (12px) — Applied to cards, buttons, and inputs for a soft, approachable feel.
- **Spacing Unit:** 8px base grid.
- **Section Gaps:** 64px to 96px for breathing room.

## UI Principles
- **Intentional Whitespace:** Use generous margins to create a sense of calm and organization.
- **Soft Shadows:** Use very subtle, warm shadows (`rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.05)`) to lift elements without harshness.
- **Natural Assets:** Prioritize photography with natural lighting and realistic textures.
